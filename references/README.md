## Rules for using these

1. **Take the structure, not the content.** ANALOG's robot, its lines ("Are you my savior?"), its blue briefing tape, its office and its printers belong to other people. This game has to be original: its own monster, story, setting, words and look.
2. **The stills here are for study only.** They are frames of someone else's video of someone else's game. Never ship them in the game, on the game page, or in thumbnails.
3. **More references may arrive.** The owner said they may send the other parts of the video later. Add them here the same way: stills, timeline, notes.
