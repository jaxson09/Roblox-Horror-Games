## The HUD, as seen at 1:05

- **Top right:** objective card — "Take 0/5 Pictures of Subject" and "Print 0/5 Pictures", each with a row of pips.
- **Mid left:** two stacked store shortcuts, "Infinite Lives" and "Hunt Player".
- **Bottom left:** NOISE METER (quiet / normal / loud) and a stamina bar.
- **Bottom centre:** a hotbar of items.
- **Bottom right:** key hints.
- **Over everything:** camcorder viewfinder brackets, a date and time stamp, and a "TURN ON VC" banner.
- **Top centre:** one large event line at a time ("IT SEES YOU. RUN").

## What makes it work

1. **The job is clear in thirty seconds.** A numbered list on a tape, then the same list as a card on the HUD. Nobody is ever lost.
2. **One loop with three beats:** get the evidence (dangerous), process the evidence (loud, slow, you are stuck in one place), leave (the building turns on you). Each beat is frightening in a different way.
3. **The monster talks to *you*.** By name, in subtitles, and through the microphone. It is the thing people clip and share.
4. **Big plain event lines.** "IT SEES YOU. RUN." tells a brand-new player exactly what is happening and what to do.
5. **A grab is an event, not a death.** A close-up, a question, a struggle. Players get several chances.
6. **Light is the art direction.** A normal-looking office, then a hard white flashlight circle with black around it. Almost monochrome.
7. **Short.** One part is about ten minutes. Easy to finish, easy to replay with friends, easy to make a video of.
8. **A reward screen and a "next part" tease** at the end.

## What this game takes, and what it must not

| Take (structure) | Do not take (content) |
| --- | --- |
| A briefing tape before the job | The blue-screen tape design and its wording |
| Photograph → process → leave | Printers, the office, "print the photographs" |
| A monster that hears noise and speaks to players by name | E.L.B.E.R.R, its skull face, its tennis-ball hands, "Are you my savior?" |
| Grab, question, break free, lives | The pink close-up and its exact layout |
| One event line at a time, objective card, noise meter | ANALOG's exact HUD arrangement, fonts and wording ("IT SEES YOU. RUN" is theirs — find our own voice) |
| Field report with XP; parts released over time | The name ANALOG, "Kioverse", the case numbers |

The robot in ANALOG is itself borrowed from another creator's animated series (*I Made A Self-Aware Robot* by LIGHTS ARE OFF). Copying it would be copying twice over.

**An honest note on the current build:** several of our event lines ("IT SEES YOU. RUN.", "IT HEARS YOU. BE QUIET.", "IT LOST YOU. STAY HIDDEN.", "THE POWER IS OUT") are word for word or nearly word for word ANALOG's. So is "DO NOT ANSWER IT." as a tagline. They should be rewritten in this game's own voice before release.
